2. Engagement & Scope of Services
Production engages DIT as an independent contractor. DIT agrees to deliver the following services:
  • Camera media offload and verified checksum backup (minimum 3 copies — MD5 or SHA-256)
  • Mag/Card logging and daily data reporting
  • Transcode camera originals to ProRes LT .MOV (preferred) or DNxHR LB .MXF at 1080p camera native aspect ratio with Show LUT applied — clean, no burn-ins
  • Deliver Show LUT files with every daily delivery
  • Deliver sound roll folders and sound reports
  • Deliver camera reports, editor's log, script supervisor notes, and DIT offload reports
  • Daily digital upload via Media Shuttle
  • Deliver Master Drive at production wrap containing: camera originals, sound rolls, transcodes, show LUTs, BTS footage/photos, VFX elements/plates, all reports
  • Format all drives / RAID to macOS compatible file system (HFS+ or APFS)
Any services not listed above are outside the scope of this Agreement. Additional services requested during production may incur additional charges, to be agreed in writing before performance.
5. Independent Contractor Status
DIT is an independent contractor and not an employee of Production. DIT is responsible for all federal, state, and local taxes on compensation received. DIT is not entitled to employee benefits, workers' compensation, or unemployment insurance from Production.
6. Intellectual Property & Footage Ownership
All camera original footage, transcodes, and production materials remain the sole intellectual property of the production company. DIT is a custodian of these materials and acquires no ownership rights. DIT shall not retain copies of camera originals, dailies, or any production material without prior written permission. DIT shall not post, publish, or share any footage, images, or production details without written approval.
7. Confidentiality
DIT agrees to keep all information related to the production strictly confidential during and after the production. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement.
8. Data Integrity & Liability
DIT will use industry-standard checksummed offload procedures (MD5 or SHA-256) and will not reformat any camera media until all agreed backup copies have passed checksum verification. DIT shall maintain offload software logs as documentation. DIT's liability for data loss shall not exceed the total compensation paid under this Agreement, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

DIT shall be compensated for all days worked through the date of termination.
