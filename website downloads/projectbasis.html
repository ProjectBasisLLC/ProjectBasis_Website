What We Do

DIT Services

On-Set Monitoring

Real-time color-accurate monitoring with calibrated displays ensuring directors and cinematographers see exactly what's being captured.

Live Color Grading

Immediate look application and creative color work on set, providing instant visual feedback for the entire production team.

Data Management

Secure, redundant backup systems with checksum verification ensuring your footage is protected from the moment it's recorded.

Quality Control

Comprehensive frame-by-frame analysis and technical QC reports delivered daily to maintain the highest production standards.

Workflow Design

Custom pipeline development tailored to your production's unique needs, from camera to post-production handoff.

Dailies Processing

Same-day editorial proxies with synced audio and applied looks, keeping your edit team on schedule.

About Us

Crafting the
Visual Foundation

Project Basis was founded on a simple principle: every frame matters. As dedicated Digital Imaging Technicians, we bridge the gap between the creative vision on set and the technical excellence required in post-production.

With years of experience across feature films, commercials, and episodic content, we bring meticulous attention to color science, data integrity, and workflow optimization to every project we touch.

Our commitment is to be invisible when things go right, and indispensable when challenges arise.
